# Presentation outline

Use this skill when the user needs the structure and the text of a talk or a slide deck.

## Steps

1. Find the audience, the time, and the goal. What must the audience think or do at the end?
2. Write the one-sentence message of the talk.
3. Choose the structure: problem, solution, proof, and ask. Or situation, complication, and answer.
4. Write one line for each slide: a full sentence that states the point of the slide, not only a topic word.
5. For each slide, suggest the visual: a chart, a diagram, a photo, or a short list.
6. Add short speaker notes with the key points and the numbers.

## Rules

- Plan about one slide for each 1 to 2 minutes.
- Use at most 6 lines on a slide and at most 6 words in a line when you can.
- Put the answer or the ask at the start and at the end.
- Use only facts and numbers that the user gave you or that you checked. Name the source on the slide.
- Offer to make the file if the sandbox has a tool for it.
