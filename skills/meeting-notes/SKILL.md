# Meeting notes

Use this skill when the user gives you a transcript, a recording note, or raw notes of a meeting.

## Steps

1. Find the date, the people, and the purpose of the meeting. If you cannot find them, write "not stated".
2. Read the whole text. Group the content by topic, not by time.
3. Write the notes in the format below.
4. Check that each action has an owner. If it has none, mark it "owner not stated". Do not invent an owner or a date.

## Format

- Title, date, and people.
- Summary: 3 sentences at most.
- Decisions: a list of what the group decided and why.
- Actions: a table with the action, the owner, and the due date.
- Open questions: what the group did not settle.
- Notes by topic: short points for the context that matters.

## Rules

- Keep what people said true. Do not change a number or a commitment.
- Remove small talk and repeats.
- When the transcript is unclear, say so in the notes. Do not fill the gap with a guess.
