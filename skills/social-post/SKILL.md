# Social post

Use this skill when the user wants a post for X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Mastodon, Instagram, or
a similar network, or a thread, or several variants to test.

## Steps

1. Get the goal (inform, announce, start a talk, get sign-ups), the audience, the network,
   and the voice of the account. Ask for two or three earlier posts that worked.
2. Find the one idea of the post. Write it as one sentence.
3. Write a first line that makes the reader stop: a concrete fact, a result, a question, or
   a short story. The first line is all that most people read.
4. Fit the network:
   - X and Bluesky: short, one idea, at most two hashtags,
   - LinkedIn: short paragraphs, a concrete lesson or result, a question at the end,
   - a thread: one point for each post, and the first post works alone.
5. Add one clear call to action when the goal needs it.
6. Give two or three variants with different first lines, when the user wants options.

## Rules

- Check the character limit of the network. Count the characters.
- Do not invent numbers, quotes, or customer stories.
- Tag a person or a company only when the user agrees.
- Do not post. Give the text to the user. Posting through a connector needs the approval of
  the user.
